World3 Advertise

Put your brand in front of verified people who are really there.

Your ad appears on the “Stamp collected” screen right after someone captures a spot at the event. Each person sees it once, and only after World ID has checked they are a unique human standing there. Your campaign is an Arkiv record owned by your wallet: World3 can decline to show it, but cannot change it, and you can pause or delete it yourself.
